# Draft information request to KSPCB

**DRAFT — not sent**

[Date]

**To:** Kerala State Pollution Control Board

**Attention:** Appropriate records/information officer

**Subject: Request for official air-emissions, facility identity, location, inspection, and consent records for eight Edayar candidate facilities**

Dear Sir/Madam,

I request copies of official records held by the Kerala State Pollution Control Board (KSPCB) concerning the eight candidate facilities listed below. The names, aliases, and address references are taken from an existing Edayar candidate-facility inventory and are included to help locate records. They are not an assertion that a facility is currently operating, has violated a condition, or is responsible for any ambient pollution. Please use KSPCB registration, consent, application, or other record identifiers to confirm the identity of any matching facility and note any name changes or uncertainty.

For each matching facility, please provide the following records **where available**:

1. **Stack-emission monitoring and test reports**
   - Complete reports and result sheets for stack-emission sampling or monitoring, including the facility and stack/monitoring-point identity; sampling and report dates; pollutants; actual measured results; units; sampling duration or averaging period; sampling and analytical methods; and relevant operating conditions recorded during the test.
   - Any non-detect or below-detection-limit results with the notation and reporting limit used. Please retain the source's original notation and units.
   - Identify applicable emission standards or consent limits separately from actual measured results. A consent limit or prescribed standard should not be presented as a measured emission value.

2. **Facility identity and site-location records**
   - KSPCB facility, consent, or application identifiers; the registered entity and premises name; and records linking any former, alternate, or trade name to that facility.
   - Official coordinates, coordinate reference system/datum, plot or site identifiers, or georeferenced maps/site plans, if held. Please identify the record and date that supports any coordinates and distinguish a facility point from an estate or area reference.

3. **Air-emission inspections and environmental monitoring**
   - Inspection, compliance-monitoring, ambient monitoring conducted at or for a facility, or other records relevant to air emissions, including dates, locations, observations, results, methods, and any recorded follow-up or corrective action.
   - Please identify the media and purpose of each monitoring record. Wastewater, trade-effluent, or other water-quality results are not stack-emission results; if such records are included for context, please label them separately.

4. **Consent-to-operate status and orders**
   - The latest status available as of the date of your response for each facility's consent-to-operate relevant to air emissions, together with copies of the relevant consent orders, renewals, amendments, variations, and any order or record showing expiry, suspension, revocation, replacement, or pending status, where held.
   - Please include issue and validity dates, the consent/record identifier, and the scope of the order. List any emission limits or conditions in a separate section from monitoring results.

5. **Monitoring authority and data quality**
   - For each monitoring report, please state whether sampling or measurement was conducted by KSPCB, the facility/operator, a laboratory, or another authority, as recorded by the source.
   - Where recorded, include the laboratory name, applicable accreditation/certification details and validity at the time of testing, sampling and analytical methods, quality-control notes, and report approval/signatory details.

Please provide records electronically where available. For tabular data, the original spreadsheet or machine-readable file is preferred; otherwise, legible copies of the source documents are welcome. Please retain the original dates, units, identifiers, qualifiers, and missing-value notation.

If a requested record or category is not held or could not be located, please identify the category and facility searched and indicate, if known, which office or authority may hold it. Please state the reporting period covered by the records provided.

Thank you for your assistance.

Yours faithfully,
