## Weather next-day research baselines (retrained 2026-10-31)

The historical weather workflow now compares Random Forest and Extra Trees using only the 2015–2019 validation period, chooses the decision threshold there, and leaves 2020 onward as the chronological test period. Current-day observations are included as predictors for next-day targets; this assumes those observations are available when a forecast would be issued.

|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Next-day high temperature at or above training-period 95th percentile (32.8 °C) | 8,768 | Random Forest | 0.754 | 0.898 | 0.858 | 0.673 | 0.971 | 0.819 | 0.057 |
| Next-day extreme rainfall at or above training-period 95th percentile (25.9 mm/day) | 8,768 | Random Forest | 0.416 | 0.685 | 0.417 | 0.415 | 0.876 | 0.382 | 0.067 |

- Training rows: 29,188 per target; data covers four historical Kerala grid locations (Kochi, Kottayam, Wayanad and Thiruvananthapuram).
- High-temperature persistence baseline: F1 0.739 and balanced accuracy 0.850. The model improves on this baseline modestly.
- Extreme-rainfall persistence baseline: F1 0.414 and balanced accuracy 0.683. The model is only marginally better on these test metrics; this is not strong evidence of useful operational forecast skill.
- The thresholds are research definitions, not IMD heatwave/rainfall-warning thresholds. These historical gridded-data scores do not establish performance at weather stations or validated local predictions for Kochi.
- The saved report includes confusion matrices, average precision, Brier scores and majority/persistence baselines. Model artifacts and reports are regenerated by `.github/workflows/train-weather-baselines.yml`.

## Flood-event severity baseline results

- Usable labeled events: 4,451 from 153 gauges; 97 of 4,548 events lacked gauge danger-level metadata and were excluded.
- Train/test: 3,098 / 1,353 events; 114 train gauges and 39 held-out gauges.
- Single gauge-holdout ROC-AUC: 0.646; balanced accuracy: 0.578; F1: 0.456.
- Additional 5-fold gauge-grouped cross-validation: mean ROC-AUC 0.633 (SD 0.120), mean balanced accuracy 0.602 (SD 0.088), mean F1 0.480 (SD 0.152).
- One cross-validation fold had ROC-AUC 0.433, showing substantial variability across held-out gauges.
- Single-holdout majority baseline accuracy: 0.611; model accuracy: 0.615.

These results show only modest discrimination. This is **not** a validated flood forecasting model. INDOFLOODS contains recorded flood events, not matched non-flood periods, so it cannot train the probability of a flood occurring. The records are from Indian gauges generally and do not establish performance in Kochi.

## Reproducibility

The training script is `scripts/train_flood_event_severity_baseline.py`. It reads the four INDOFLOODS tables from `data/external/raw/`, performs a gauge-held-out test plus five-fold grouped validation, fits a final research model on all 4,451 labeled events, saves it to `models/flood_event_severity_baseline.joblib`, and writes metrics to `reports/flood_event_severity_baseline.json`.

Source: Kuntla & Saharia, INDOFLOODS, Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.14584654. Dataset is described as openly accessible for academic/research purposes; cite the dataset and associated paper and observe original-source data policies.

**Safety gate:** Do not label all ten hazards “trained” or expose this baseline as a live warning model. Each hazard needs its own target, appropriate observations, leakage-resistant validation, calibration/uncertainty analysis, and independent validation before scientific prediction claims.
