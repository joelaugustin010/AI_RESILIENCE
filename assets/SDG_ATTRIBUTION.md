# Sustainable Development Goal artwork

The homepage uses the official individual SDG web icons supplied by the United Nations
Sustainable Development communications materials page:

<https://www.un.org/sustainabledevelopment/news/communications-material/>

The six files in `app/static/images/sdg/` are the English web icon assets downloaded from
the official Goal 3, 6, 9, 11, 13 and 15 packages. They are used without the UN emblem,
with the required link to the UN SDG website and the non-endorsement disclaimer displayed
in the application. The icons are kept in their original artwork and colours.

This project does not claim certification, endorsement or achievement of any SDG.
