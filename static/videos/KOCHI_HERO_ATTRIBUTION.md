# Cinematic hero footage

`kochi-chinese-fishing-nets-480p.webm` is a 480p transcode of [Chinese Fishing
nets.webm on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chinese_Fishing_nets.webm),
by Thomas Kallarackal, licensed CC BY 3.0.

- Location: Kochi, Kerala, India
- Author: Thomas Kallarackal
- Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XNyLWCx05Cw
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
- Local file: reduced-resolution transcode for the homepage hero

`vembanad-boat-480p.webm` is a 480p transcode of [Motor boat sailing in
Vembanad lake](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Motor_boat_sailing_in_Vembanad_lake.webm)
by Subhashish Panigrahi, licensed CC BY-SA 3.0.

`kerala-flood-2018-480p.webm` is a 480p transcode of [Kerala Flood 2018
Cherumkavala, Angamaly](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kerala_Flood_2018_Cherumkavala,_Angamaly.webm)
by Ranjithsiji, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. It is labelled as Kerala flood context,
not as a current Kochi warning or a current Kochi event.
